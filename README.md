# HR Employee Attrition Analysis (Power BI) – Project Summary
Project Overview

Developed an interactive HR Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard in Power BI to identify the primary drivers behind employee turnover and support HR leadership in making data-driven retention decisions. The dashboard analyzes employee demographics, department-wise attrition, overtime impact, salary distribution, job satisfaction, job roles, and age groups to uncover high-risk employee segments.

The dashboard enables HR managers to monitor attrition trends through dynamic slicers (Age Group, Gender, Department, Marital Status, Education Field, and Job Role), making it easier to identify workforce patterns and prioritize retention strategies.

Business Problem

Employee attrition increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and impacts organizational performance. HR required a centralized dashboard to:

* Monitor employee turnover
* Identify departments with high attrition
* Understand factors affecting resignations
* Improve employee retention strategy
* Enable management to make proactive HR decisions

-> Overall Business Insights

* Overall employee attrition stands at 16.12%.
* Sales department contributes the largest share of resignations.
* Sales Representatives exhibit the highest attrition rate (39.76%).
* Employees aged 18–25 are the most vulnerable group (35.77%).
* Overtime increases attrition by nearly 3×.
* Lower-income employees leave at significantly higher rates.
* Employees with low job satisfaction demonstrate the greatest resignation risk.
* Leadership roles (Managers, Research Directors) have relatively stable retention.

- Strategic Recommendations

* Workforce Management
* Reduce excessive overtime through workload balancing.
* Improve staffing levels in Sales and HR.
* Monitor overtime trends monthly.
  
- Compensation

* Review salary bands for low-income employees.
* Introduce retention bonuses for high-risk operational roles.
* Benchmark compensation against market standards.

- Employee Engagement

* Conduct quarterly employee satisfaction surveys.
* Improve manager feedback mechanisms.
* Recognize employee achievements more frequently.

- Career Development

* Launch mentorship programs for employees aged 18–25.
* Define transparent career progression paths.
* Increase internal promotion opportunities.
* Department-Level Focus

Prioritize retention initiatives for:

- Sales Representatives
- Laboratory Technicians
- Human Resources
- Sales Executives
- Business Impact

This dashboard enables HR leaders to:
